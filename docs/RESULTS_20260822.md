tree. They are included as engineering evidence, not as physical-robot claims.

## Six-axis visual closed loop

Command:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m smartpick_vla evaluate-vision `
  --config configs/eval/vision_guided_six_axis.yaml `
  --checkpoint checkpoints/vision_six_axis_release_v2/best.pt `
```

perception randomization, a nine-point simulated calibration fixture, a learned

## Resumable industrial matrix

Command:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m smartpick_vla industrial-evaluate `
  --config configs/eval/industrial_smoke.yaml `
  --include-expert `
  --method temporal_vla=checkpoints/upgrade_20260815/temporal_vla_mission_six_axis/best.pt `
  --output results/industrial_smoke
```

The matrix contains two mission lengths, three suites, two seeds, and two
methods: 24 episode rows over 12 task/suite/seed units. A second identical run
resumed all 24 rows and executed zero new rows, proving the resume key and
manifest behavior. The learned Temporal VLA smoke checkpoint did not succeed
in this budget; that negative result is retained in the summary rather than
hidden behind the expert baseline.

## ONNX deployment parity

The six-axis RGB localizer checkpoint was exported to
`outputs/vision_six_axis_release_v2.onnx` and verified with ONNX Runtime.
Maximum absolute differences against PyTorch were:

- center output: `3.58e-7`
- visibility output: `1.19e-6`

The sidecar manifest records the checkpoint hash, ONNX hash, input/output
contract, runtime versions, and `physical_robot_execution: false`.
